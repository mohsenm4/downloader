# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25

First public release.

- Download **Audio** (mp3), **Video**, or **Text** (audio + transcript) from a link. Many links at once, one per line.
- Speech to text from your own audio or video files: drop them on the window or choose a file.
- Queue with progress for every job; stop, retry, open the text, or show the file in Finder.
- Speech to text with whisper.cpp, offline. Language: Auto, Persian or English. Optional English translation.
- Output next to the media file: `.txt` and `.srt`.
- Plain-language errors for common yt-dlp problems, with the full message one click away.
- Audio mode downloads only the audio stream; playlist links download only the one video.
- Stopping a job or closing the window also stops yt-dlp, ffmpeg and whisper.
- Light and dark theme; remembers your last choices.
- Universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
